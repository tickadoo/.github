# Security Policy

Security is important to tickadoo. We welcome responsible reports that help us protect our customers, partners, team, and services.

## Report a vulnerability

Email **hello@tickadoo.com** with the subject line **[Security] Vulnerability report**.

Please include, where possible:

- the affected service, domain, repository, endpoint, or version;
- a clear description of the issue and its potential impact;
- reproducible steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- any relevant request IDs, timestamps, screenshots, or logs; and
- how you would like to be credited, if at all.

Please do not report suspected vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, pull requests, Slack channels, or social media.

We aim to acknowledge reports within two business days and provide an initial assessment within five business days. These are response targets rather than guarantees. We will keep reporters informed while we investigate and remediate confirmed issues.

## Scope

This policy covers tickadoo-owned and operated websites, applications, APIs, infrastructure, integrations, and source-code repositories.

Only the current production service and supported release branches are actively maintained. If a repository is an unmodified mirror or fork of a third-party project, please report an upstream-only vulnerability to that project's maintainers. Please still contact us when the issue affects a tickadoo deployment, configuration, integration, or modification.

## Responsible research

When testing, please:

- use accounts and data that you own or have permission to use;
- minimise access to data and stop immediately if you encounter personal, confidential, payment, authentication, or other sensitive information;
- do not copy, retain, alter, delete, or publicly disclose data;
- do not disrupt services or degrade availability;
- do not perform denial-of-service testing, social engineering, phishing, spam, physical attacks, or high-volume automated scanning;
- do not exploit a vulnerability beyond what is necessary to demonstrate it safely;
- protect vulnerability details and any evidence from unauthorised access; and
- allow us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate before disclosure.

If you believe urgent action is needed to prevent harm, say so clearly in the report.

## Safe harbour

If you act in good faith, follow this policy, avoid harm, and comply with applicable law, tickadoo will treat your research as authorised for the purpose of identifying and reporting security vulnerabilities. We will not pursue or support legal action against you solely for that research.

This safe harbour applies only to systems controlled by tickadoo. It does not authorise testing of third-party systems, suppliers, customer environments, or data without their permission.

## Rewards

tickadoo does not currently operate a paid bug-bounty programme. Please do not make disclosure conditional on payment. We may acknowledge helpful reporters with their permission.
